# Visual Novel Project

## 1. Core Concept

A soft and heart-aching emotional visual novel set in a school setting.

This game starts with warm daily moments, cute interactions, and gentle romance.
As the story progresses, each heroine's hidden pain begins to surface, and the protagonist is forced to confront emotions he tried to ignore.

### One-line Pitch
A gentle school romance visual novel where getting closer to each heroine reveals the pain hidden behind their smile.

## 2. Tone & Mood

- Soft and warm atmosphere
- Cute daily-life interactions
- Emotional pain that builds slowly
- Heart-aching character routes
- Lingering endings with strong emotional aftertaste

### Emotional Flow
- Early: comfort, humor, sweetness
- Mid: subtle unease, emotional tension
- Late: painful truths, emotional climax, choice and consequence

## 3. Main Character

### Protagonist
- Temporary Name: Hajun
- Personality: kind, calm, emotionally restrained
- Trait: understands others well, but avoids facing his own pain
- Theme: healing others while learning to confront himself

## 4. Heroines

### Yuna
- Role: bright and cute heroine, daily-life warmth
- Personality: cheerful, playful, comforting
- Hidden Pain: pressures herself to always smile
- Route Theme: "Why does someone so bright feel so fragile?"

### Sera
- Role: white-haired, red-eyed mystery heroine
- Personality: quiet, distant, delicate
- Hidden Pain: afraid of emotional closeness
- Route Theme: "Someone you want to reach, but cannot easily touch"

### Sohee
- Role: teasing and realistic emotional heroine
- Personality: playful, blunt, easy to talk to
- Hidden Pain: hides her wounds behind jokes
- Route Theme: "The one who says she's fine is often the most broken"

## 5. Story Structure

### Common Route
The protagonist meets the three heroines and slowly becomes involved in their daily lives.
Warm and comforting moments gradually reveal emotional cracks beneath the surface.

### Route Structure
- Character routes branch through affection points and key flags
- Each heroine route explores a different kind of pain and emotional intimacy
- A true ending unlocks after clearing all main heroine routes

## 6. Core Systems

- Choice-based branching
- Affection system
- Route flags
- Multiple endings
- True ending unlock condition

## 7. Development Direction

### Genre
- School romance
- Emotional drama
- Light mystery

### Platform
- PC first

### Estimated Playtime
- 4 to 6 hours for first full version

### Target Feeling
The player should smile often, then suddenly feel their chest tighten without noticing when it started.
